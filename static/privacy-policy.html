Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 26, 2025

1. Introduction

Tama is an open-source project operated by Federico Curzel. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding data collection and use of our service.

2. Data Collection

We do not collect, record, share, or store any personal data or user information. Tama operates without tracking or monitoring user activities.

3. User-Generated Content

Users can create and share content through Tama. Any content you create is stored locally or on servers you configure. We do not monitor, analyze, or collect data about your content creation activities.

4. Federated Service and Third-Party Content

Tama operates as a federated service that may include content indexes and servers operated by third parties. These third-party services:

  • Have been vetted before inclusion in our default index
  • Operate under their own privacy policies which we do not control
  • May have different data collection practices

We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party servers or content providers.

5. Custom Server Configuration

Users can configure custom server lists through the CLI or mobile app settings. When using custom servers:

  • You assume all responsibility for the servers you connect to
  • We decline any responsibility for the privacy practices of custom servers
  • You should review the privacy policies of any custom servers you use

6. Open Source

Tama is an open-source project. You can review our code to verify our privacy practices at any time.

7. Content Reporting

Users can report content from other users. Reports are handled by the server operators (whether default or third-party), not by us centrally.

8. Changes to This Policy

We reserve the right to modify this Privacy Policy at any time. Changes will be effective immediately upon posting. Your continued use of Tama constitutes acceptance of any changes.

9. Future Changes

As Tama evolves, including potential monetization features, this Privacy Policy may be updated accordingly. We will notify users of significant changes.

10. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact: Federico Curzel via the project repository.
